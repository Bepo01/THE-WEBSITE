Why this camera
Where it started
The D40x was my entry into serious photography. Lightweight, capable, and forgiving enough to learn on — but demanding enough to teach real discipline with light and manual settings.
The glass
F-Mount legacy
The Nikon F-Mount has existed since 1959. This body accepts lenses from any decade of Nikon's history — a 50mm f/1.8 from the 80s renders just as beautifully as anything modern.
The rendering
CCD character
10 megapixels is more than enough. The CCD sensor produces a colour rendering quality that modern CMOS sensors still try to replicate. Old tech. Genuine results.
